# Privacy Policy - Dobar

**Last Updated:** February 10, 2026

**Dobar Tecnologia** ("we," "us," or "Dobar") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application, website, and services ("Services"), in compliance with the General Data Protection Law (LGPD), GDPR, and other applicable laws.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services:

-   **Personal Information:** Name, email, phone number, date of birth, and profile picture (if provided).
-   **Payment Information:** Credit/debit card data is processed and stored securely directly by our payment partners (Pagar.me, Stripe, etc.). Dobar only stores encrypted tokens to facilitate future purchases.
-   **Location:** We collect your precise location (GPS) when you use the App to suggest nearby establishments and verify if you are at the location for certain services (check-in, promotions).
-   **Device Information:** Device model, operating system, unique identifiers, and IP address.
-   **Usage Data:** Order history, interactions with promotions, and preferences.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:
-   **Process Orders:** Send your order to the bar/restaurant and process payment.
-   **Personalization:** Recommend places and promotions based on your location and history.
-   **Communication:** Send receipts, order updates, and, if you opt-in, marketing and special offers.
-   **Security:** Detect and prevent fraud and illegal activities.
-   **Service Improvement:** Analyze aggregated data to understand trends and improve the platform.

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as necessary:
-   **With Partners (Bars/Restaurants):** We share your name and order details so they can prepare and deliver your order.
-   **Service Providers:** Payment processors, hosting services (Fly.io, AWS), and analytics tools.
-   **Legal Obligation:** If required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.

## 4. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. We use encryption (SSL/TLS) for data transmission.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:
-   **Access:** Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
-   **Correction:** Request correction of incomplete or incorrect data.
-   **Deletion:** Request deletion of your account and personal data, except when retention is required by law (e.g., tax records).
-   **Revocation of Consent:** Withdraw your consent for location use or marketing at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact us via email: privacy@dobar.app or through the application settings.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for minors under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware of this, we will take steps to delete such information.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The latest version will always be available on the App and Website.

**Questions?**
