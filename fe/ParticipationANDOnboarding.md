# BarZ Participation & Onboarding Agreement

This BarZ Participation & Onboarding Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into on the **Effective Date** by and between:

- **BarZ LLC** (“BarZ,” “Platform,” or “Provider”), with principal place of business at __________________;  
- The **Bar / Restaurant Partner** (“Bar,” “Merchant,” or “Partner”), with principal place at _________________, duly organized and authorized to do business under applicable laws.

BarZ and Partner may each be referred to herein as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

1.1 BarZ will provide the Partner with access to its mobile/web ordering platform (“Platform”) to receive, manage, and fulfill orders placed by customers via the BarZ client apps.  
1.2 During the **Pilot Period** (as defined below), BarZ shall provide setup, hardware (monitor loan, if applicable), onboarding, and support services.  
1.3 After the Pilot Period, the Parties may transition to a commercial arrangement for ongoing use, subject to new terms or upgrades.

---

## 2. Term & Pilot Period

2.1 **Pilot Period**: Commences on the Effective Date and continues for **___ days** (e.g. 90 days) or until mutual termination.  
2.2 Upon expiration of the Pilot Period, this Agreement will automatically convert to a recurring term (e.g. month-to-month or annual) under the commercial terms in **Exhibit A** unless either party provides written notice at least **30 days** in advance.

---

## 3. Services & Onboarding

3.1 BarZ shall provide onboarding services, including menu import, hardware setup, staff training, and promotional material (“Onboarding Services”).  
3.2 The Bar shall cooperate fully, provide necessary documentation and access, and deploy signage/QR codes as instructed.  
3.3 BarZ grants to the Bar a non-exclusive, revocable license to use the Platform solely for its storefront during the Term.

---

## 4. Fees, Commission & Payment

4.1 **Commission / Transaction Fee**: Partner agrees to pay BarZ a commission equal to **__%** of each gross order amount (excluding taxes, service fees).  
4.2 **Subscription / SaaS Fee** (if applicable after pilot): The commercial plan in Exhibit A.  
4.3 **Hardware / Monitor Fee**: If hardware is loaned, terms are in **Exhibit B**.  
4.4 BarZ shall settle net payments to Partner’s bank account on a **weekly/biweekly/monthly** basis (choose one), minus commissions and fees.  
4.5 Late payments not disputed in writing within **10 business days** are subject to interest at **1.5% per month** or the maximum legal rate.

---

## 5. Data & Usage Rights

5.1 BarZ collects order data, transaction history, user behavior data. BarZ and Partner may use aggregated data for analytics, forecasting, and improvement.  
5.2 BarZ may publicly identify the Partner as a merchant on its platform for marketing purposes, subject to Logo Usage guidelines.  
5.3 All IP (software, UI, modifications) remains the property of BarZ. Partner does not acquire ownership.

---

## 6. Termination

6.1 Either party may terminate upon material breach by the other, if uncured within **30 days’ notice**.  
6.2 BarZ may suspend service if Partner fails to pay undisputed amounts after **10 business days’ notice**.  
6.3 Upon termination: Partner must remove signage/QR, stop using the Platform, and return any loaned hardware within **30 days**.

---

## 7. Representations & Warranties; Liability

7.1 Each party has authority to enter this Agreement.  
7.2 BarZ provides the Platform “as is” and does not warrant continuous availability or error-free operation.  
7.3 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, special or consequential damages. Total liability capped at the total commission paid in the prior 3 months.  
7.4 Partner shall indemnify BarZ against claims related to food safety, local regulatory noncompliance, or conduct in their premises.

---

## 8. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

8.1 Each party shall keep confidential business information (pricing, financials, trade secrets) and shall not disclose to third parties without consent.  
8.2 Obligations survive termination for **2 years**.

---

## 9. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of **[State / Country]**.  
9.2 Disputes shall be resolved via binding arbitration in **[City / Region]**, or court jurisdiction as mutually agreed.

---

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 Notices must be in writing (email or registered mail).  
10.2 Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.  
10.3 If any provision is held invalid, remaining provisions stand.  
10.4 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement and supersedes earlier oral or written statements.
