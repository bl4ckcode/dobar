# 🟡 Dobar Brand Identity & Marketing Strategy

> **Status:** Final / Approved
> **Last Updated:** January 24, 2026

---

## 🎨 Brand Identity

### Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** To be the default operating system for nightlife commerce.
- **Mission:** Empowering bar owners with seamless payments and high-impact marketing tools.

### Personality & Voice
- **Bold & Energetic:** High-contrast, vibrant, and standout.
- **Reliable:** Essential and consistent like a perfectly poured pint.
- **Industrial Modern:** Gritty brewery aesthetics meet digital-first precision.

### Color Palette
- **Dobar Electric Yellow:** `#FFD700` (Energy, Beer, Visibility).
- **Deep Onyx Black:** `#0A0A0A` (Nightlife, Sophistication, Solid).

---

## 🏷️ Visual Assets (Identity)

### Logo Strategy
1. **Primary Logo (The Symmetrical Flow):** Modern wordmark with symmetrical 'd' and 'b' and a unique split 'o'. This is the core brand asset.
2. **Inverted Variant:** The primary logo in deep black on an electric yellow background. Used for high-contrast applications.
3. **Auxiliary Logo (The Tap Flow):** Stylized 'd' with a tap/drop. Used specifically for brewery-focused collateral or physical tap handles.

### Mockups
- **Brewery Integration:** Vertical high-contrast marketing dashboards in situ.
- **Signage:** High-visibility yellow-on-black metal signage for outdoor/indoor environments.

---

## 📈 Marketing & Positioning

### Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
**"The Pulse of the Brewery."**
Integrated marketing that lives where the consumption happens. Growth driven by data, not just transactions.

### Target Segments
- Craft Breweries & Taprooms
- High-end Nightclubs & Lounges
- Sports Bars (Promotion Boost focus)
- Outdoor Beer Gardens
