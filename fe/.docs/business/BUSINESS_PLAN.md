## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Dobar** is a live commerce infrastructure platform designed for bars, restaurants, and mass events. We provide a unified mobile ordering and payment solution that eliminates queues, reduces transaction costs, and increases venue revenue.

### The Opportunity

| Market Reality | Impact |
|----------------|--------|
| 30-45 min queue wait at peak times | Lost sales, frustrated customers |
| 15-18% traditional payment fees (maquininha + aggregators) | Margin erosion for venues |
| Fragmented POS systems | No unified customer experience |
| No real-time inventory at events | Stockouts, disappointed customers |

### Our Solution

A three-pillar ecosystem:
1. **Dobar App** - Consumer mobile ordering
2. **Dobar Payment Engine** - Multi-gateway middleware (PIX, cards, wallets)
3. **Dobar Connect** - Event connectivity infrastructure (future)

### Cost Comparison: Dobar vs Traditional Payment

| Payment Method | Traditional "Maquininha" | Dobar Platform |
|----------------|--------------------------|----------------|
| **PIX** | R$ 0 (but no integration) | R$ 0 (fully integrated) |
| **Credit Card** | 4.99% + D+30 settlement | 3.49% + instant routing |
| **Debit Card** | 2.49% + D+1 | 1.99% + D+1 |
| **Monthly Rental** | R$ 49-149/month | R$ 0 (included in commission) |
| **POS Hardware** | R$ 300-800 one-time | R$ 0 (phone-based) |
| **Integration** | None (manual entry) | Full menu/inventory sync |
| **Analytics** | Basic/None | Full dashboard included |

**Effective Savings per R$ 10,000 in transactions:**

| Tier | Traditional Cost | Dobar Cost | Savings |
|------|------------------|------------|---------|
| Regular (18% commission) | R$ 500 (fees + rental) | R$ 1,800 | -R$ 1,300* |
| Master (14% + R$ 199/mo) | R$ 500 | R$ 1,599 | -R$ 1,099* |
| VIP (10% + R$ 599/mo) | R$ 500 | R$ 1,599 | -R$ 1,099* |

*Commission includes: ordering system, menu management, analytics, marketing tools, customer support. Traditional solutions only provide payment processing.

**Break-even Analysis:**
- At R$ 15,000/month GMV, VIP tier becomes cost-neutral vs traditional
- At R$ 30,000/month GMV, VIP tier saves R$ 900/month

### Our Differentiation

1. **Nightlife Focus** - Built for bars, not restaurants
2. **Payment Engine** - Multi-gateway routing saves money
3. **Event Scale** - Architecture for 100K+ concurrent users
4. **Lower Take Rate** - 10-18% vs 25-30% delivery apps
5. **No Delivery Dependency** - In-venue pickupmodel
