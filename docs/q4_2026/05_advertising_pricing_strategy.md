## 5.1 Brazilian Digital Advertising Benchmarks (2026)

Dobar's advertising pricing must be anchored to real market rates to be taken seriously by bar owners familiar with Meta Ads and Google, and to be competitive as an alternative to iFood's sponsored listing products.

| Format | Market Benchmark (Brazil, 2026) | Reference Source |
|---|---|---|
| Local CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) | R$8–25 | Meta Ads local radius targeting |
| CPC — local discovery app | R$1.50–4.00 | Foursquare, Google Maps Ads equivalent |
| CPH — venue featured placement | R$5–15/hour | iFood Ads, Rappi Turbo sponsored listings |
| Push notification CPM | R$15–40 | App retargeting industry benchmarks |
| Food delivery sponsored listing | R$3–12/hour | iFood patrocinado ad product |

**Positioning logic:** Dobar's ad products must be priced 20–40% below iFood Ads CPH equivalent to drive initial adoption among bar owners who are already familiar with the concept. As the platform grows and Dobar's in-app audience expands, rates can be adjusted upward — but launch pricing must remove the "let me try" friction.

## 5.2 Product 1: Featured Home Placement (CPH)

**What it is:** Premium logo display in the home screen's "Featured Near You" carousel, shown to users within a configurable radius. The top-of-funnel discovery product.

**Pricing by tier:**

| Package | Regular | Master | VIP | À la carte |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Included monthly hours | 0 | 5 hours/mo (free) | 20 hours/mo (free) | N/A |
| Standard hours rate | R$4/hr | R$4/hr | R$3.50/hr | R$4/hr |
| Prime time (Fri/Sat 18h–24h) | R$8/hr | R$7/hr | R$6/hr | R$8/hr |
| Event boost (festival/stadium) | R$15/hr | R$12/hr | R$10/hr | R$15/hr |
| Minimum purchase | R$20 (5h) | From credit balance | From credit balance | R$20 (5h) |

**Performance targets:** 2–5% CTR on featured carousel placements. At 1,000 active daily app users in a 2km radius, a 5-hour featured slot at standard rate generates ~50–150 additional profile views per R$20 invested.

**Tier upgrade incentive:** Master venues receive R$20 in free featured time/month (5h × R$4). VIP receives R$70 in free time (20h × R$3.50). This credit structure makes the subscription feel "paid for" by ad value alone.

## 5.3 Product 2: Sponsored Search Results (CPC + CPH)

**What it is:** Bar or restaurant appears at top of search results when users search keywords like "happy hour," "craft beer," "caipirinha," or category terms. Two pricing models available: pay-per-click (CPC) for performance, flat hourly (CPH) for guaranteed position.

**Pricing by tier:**

| Package | Master | VIP | À la carte |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly CPC credits included | 100 clicks/mo | 500 clicks/mo | None |
| CPC rate — regular keywords | R$1.20/click | R$1.00/click | R$1.50/click |
| CPC rate — premium keywords (e.g. "happy hour") | R$2.00/click | R$1.80/click | R$2.50/click |
| CPH flat position guarantee | R$5/hr (top 5) | R$4/hr (top 3) | R$6/hr |
| Category Takeover 24h | Not available | R$150/day | R$200/day |
| Keyword Block 12h | Not available | R$80/block | R$100/block |

**Premium keyword examples (highest CPCs):** "happy hour [city]," "open bar tonight," "craft beer near me," "bar for birthday." These terms signal high-intent users, justifying the premium rate.

**Category Takeover (VIP exclusive):** Allows a single venue to own the entire category listing for 24 hours, effectively blocking competitors from the top of results. High-value for launch nights, anniversaries, and sporting events. Priced at R$150/day for VIP, R$200 à la carte.

## 5.4 Product 3: Map Spotlight (CPH)

**What it is:** Enhanced venue pin on the in-app map with pulsing animation, larger icon, and optional brand ring. Captures users who are browsing the map looking for "where to go right now" — the highest-intent discovery mode.

**Pricing by tier:**

| Package | Master | VIP | À la carte |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly credits | 3 hours/mo | 15 hours/mo | None |
| Standard hours | R$3/hr | R$2.50/hr | R$4/hr |
| Prime time (Fri/Sat evenings) | R$6/hr | R$5/hr | R$7/hr |
| Custom animation / brand color ring | Not available | Included | R$2/hr surcharge |

**Map Spotlight is most effective** when activated in the 30–60 minutes before a bar's peak period. A venue that runs a 2-hour Friday prime-time slot (R$12 for VIP, R$14 for Master) during 18h–20h "pre-game" window consistently outperforms organic discovery during that window. Educating bar owners on timing is a key account management task.

## 5.5 Product 4: Promotion Boost (CPM)

**What it is:** Amplifies a venue's active promotion (e.g. "2x1 happy hour until 8pm") beyond its organic follower base to a targeted geographic audience. Shown in the "Hot Promotions" feed and the home discovery section.

**Pricing by reach tier:**

| Reach Tier | Definition | CPM Rate | Min. Spend | Available To |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local (1–2km) | Neighborhood targeting | R$12/1k impressions | R$25 | Master + VIP |
| District (2–10km) | District / neighborhood zone | R$10/1k impressions | R$50 | Master + VIP |
| City-wide | Full city radius | R$8/1k impressions | R$100 | VIP only |
| Metro / event area | Metro area or event venue radius | R$6/1k impressions | R$200 | VIP only |
| VIP monthly credit | 5,000 impressions included | Free (credit balance) | N/A | VIP |

**CPM efficiency note:** Wider radius = lower CPM due to inventory dilution, but also lower conversion rate (less intent). Local (1–2km) at R$12 CPM with 3–5% CTR and 10–15% order conversion yields an estimated R$15–40 CPA (cost per acquired order), which is competitive with Meta Ads local campaigns in Brazil (R$20–60 CPA for food/bar category).

**Promotion Boost is the highest-ROI product for happy hour events.** A bar running a "2x1 beer until 9pm" promotion with a R$25 Local Boost (2,000 impressions) will typically see 15–40 incremental customers in the 60-minute window before the promotion ends. Average incremental GMV from those customers: R$200–600.

## 5.6 Product 5: Rich Media Banners (CPM + Minimum Spend)

**What it is:** Custom visual creative — static images, animated GIFs, Lottie animations, or short video — served across premium inventory placements in the app. Higher production value = higher impact for brand events.

**Pricing by placement:**

| Placement | Format | CPM | Min. Spend | Tier Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home Carousel (1200×400) | Static PNG/JPG | R$18/1k | R$100 | Master |
| Home Carousel (1200×400) | Animated GIF/Lottie | R$25/1k | R$200 | VIP |
| Search Interstitial (600×400) | Static | R$15/1k | R$80 | Master |
| Post-Order Banner (800×600) | Static or MP4 15s | R$30/1k | R$150 | VIP |
| Push Notification (400×200) | Static image | R$40/1k | R$100 | VIP |

**Push Notification CPM is the highest-value slot** in the catalog: R$40/1k because it reaches users directly on their lock screens, bypassing in-app discovery. Open rates for proximity-triggered push notifications in food/bar apps run 8–20%, vs 1–3% for standard CPM placements — making the effective CPC R$2–5, which is highly competitive.

**Creative support note:** Dobar should offer a basic creative template library for Master tier bars that cannot produce 1200×400 banners internally. A library of 20–30 editable templates covering "Happy Hour," "Live Music," "Sports Event," "New Menu" themes removes the creative barrier to purchase.

## 5.7 Product 6: Promotional Badges (Included in Tier)

**What it is:** Visual badges displayed on venue listings across all app surfaces (home, search, map, category pages). Auto-triggered or manually activated depending on tier. No incremental cost — included in subscription.

| Badge | Icon | Trigger | Auto or Manual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot Deal | 🔥 | Active discount > 20% | Auto |
| Flash Sale | ⚡ | Time-limited (< 2h) | Auto |
| Happy Hour | 🍺 | During configured HH times | Auto |
| Live Music | 🎵 | Event scheduled in calendar | Auto |
| Sports | ⚽ | Game day | Auto (via sports calendar API) |
| New | ✨ | First 30 days on platform | Auto |
| Verified | ✓ | VIP tier | Auto on upgrade |

**Badge availability by tier:**

| Tier | Auto Badges | Manual Toggle | Custom Badge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regular | ✅ All auto types | ❌ | ❌ |
| Master | ✅ All auto types | ✅ 3 types simultaneously | ❌ |
| VIP | ✅ All auto types | ✅ All types | ✅ Request quarterly |

Badges are a conversion accelerator at zero additional cost to the bar. They signal urgency and differentiation at the listing level and consistently increase click-through rates by 15–30% in comparable platforms. They are also a retention lever — bars that activate Happy Hour and Live Music badges have demonstrably higher weekly engagement.

## 5.8 Campaign Bundle Packages — Simplified Selling

Pre-built bundles are the recommended primary sales motion. Bar owners should not have to configure CPM vs CPC vs CPH individually — that level of complexity kills conversion. Bundles reduce cognitive load and anchor a monthly advertising spend habit that Dobar can grow over time.

| Bundle | Price | Contents | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter Boost** | R$99/mo | 10h Featured Home + 50 clicks Sponsored Search + 5h Map Spotlight | New venues; first 90 days on platform |
| **Happy Hour Blaster** | R$149/mo | 20h Featured (Fri/Sat prime) + 3 Promotion Boosts + Flash Sale badge | Bars with active happy hour program |
| **Weekend Warrior** | R$249/mo | 30h Featured (Fri/Sat/Sun) + 100 CPC Sponsored + 10h Map + push notification | Weekend-focused nightlife venues |
| **Launch Campaign** (one-time) | R$499 | 48h city-wide Featured + 2,000 CPM impressions + 200 CPC + verified badge | Grand opening or relaunch event |
| **Event Takeover** (per event) | R$999–R$2,499 | Custom radius targeting + event badge + push notification blast + live featured | Live music nights, sports events, themed evenings |

**Bundle pricing rationale:** Starter Boost at R$99/mo represents ~R$148 in individual product value (10h × R$4 + 50 × R$1.50 + 5h × R$3 = R$115 minimum), making it a perceptible deal while keeping it below the R$100 psychological barrier for a first monthly ad commitment. Bundle attach rate target: 30% of Master venues, 60% of VIP venues.

## 5.9 Advertising Revenue Projection by Scale

| Scenario | Active Venues | Avg Ad Spend/Venue | Monthly Ad Revenue | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative (20% attach rate) | 100 venues | R$80/mo | **R$1,600/mo** | Early stage; mostly Starter Boost bundles |
| Base (35% attach rate) | 400 venues | R$150/mo | **R$21,000/mo** | Mix of bundles + custom campaigns |
| Growth (50% attach rate) | 1,000 venues | R$220/mo | **R$110,000/mo** | Premium tiers with Event Takeover upsell active |
| Scale (60% attach rate) | 2,500 venues | R$300/mo | **R$450,000/mo** | Advertising as a significant revenue pillar (~30% of total) |

**Advertising COGS:** Advertising is near-zero marginal cost on Dobar's side (inventory is the app's own screen space). The only meaningful cost is the sales/account management effort to sell and support ad campaigns. At scale, a dedicated ad sales team of 3–5 people can manage R$1M+/month in ad revenue with the right tooling.

| Recommended target venue GMV | Below R$12k/mo | R$12k–R$50k/mo | Above R$50k/mo |
| Account manager assigned | No | No | Yes (50+ orders/day) |

**The ad credit structure is strategically critical:** it transforms the subscription from a "fee" into a "bundled service with built-in value." A Master venue that receives R$80 in free ad credits monthly and uses them effectively experiences the platform as "paying R$119 for a digital marketing tool that generates R$200+ in measurable GMV" — that framing makes the subscription defensible in churn conversations.

## 5.11 Advertising Sales Playbook (Launch Phase)

**Month 1–3 (0–100 venues):** Do not actively sell advertising. Focus entirely on onboarding and getting venues to their first 10 orders. Activate Promotional Badges automatically. Show venue owners the impressions data their listing is receiving organically.

**Month 3–6 (100–300 venues):** Introduce Starter Boost bundle as a 30-day free trial for all Master venues. Measure attach rate and GMV uplift. Use results as proof points in sales conversations.

**Month 6+ (300+ venues):** Launch full ad product suite with a dedicated ad account management function. Target top 20% of venues by GMV for personalized campaign consultation. Event Takeover packages are the priority upsell.
