## 4.1 What Traditional Solutions Really Cost a Bar

To price competitively, we need the true all-in monthly cost of the status quo — not just the maquininha headline rate. A bar typically runs three cost layers simultaneously. The table below deconstructs each layer honestly:

| Cost Component | Low End | Typical Mid-Size Bar | High End | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maquininha rental (Cielo/Rede/GetNet) | R$0 (Ton / own device) | R$80–100/mo | R$150/mo | Corporate providers charge rental; Ton and Stone are purchase-based |
| PDV / comanda system | R$100/mo | R$250–400/mo | R$800/mo | Goomer R$40–699; Sischef R$350–800; Takeat R$300–800 |
| Fiscal module NFC-e | Included | R$30–80/mo add-on | R$80/mo | Often a separate add-on to the base system price |
| Maquininha credit fee (commercial) | 0.75% (Ton) | 2.5–3.0% | 4.30% (Cielo small) | Card-present; negotiable by volume above R$50k/mo |
| PIX fee | 0% (Ton / MercadoPago) | 0–0.35% | 0.35% | Most low-cost providers offer free PIX in 2026 |
| POS hardware (amortized) | R$0 (phone-based) | R$15–25/mo | R$35/mo | One-time R$300–800 amortized over 2–3 years |
| Marketing / customer acquisition | R$0 (word of mouth) | R$500–2,000/mo | R$5,000+/mo | Facebook/Instagram local ads needed to compete |

**Key takeaway:** The "maquininha" framing that bars use understates their real cost by 3–5x. A mid-size bar spending R$300 on PDV + R$1,500 on marketing + R$390 on payment fees is already paying R$2,190/month — before counting the revenue they're not generating from queue friction.

## 4.2 True Total Cost of Ownership — Traditional vs Dobar

Calculation baseline: mid-size bar with **R$30,000/month GMV**, transaction mix 55% PIX / 35% credit / 10% debit. Traditional blended payment rate: ~1.3% (card-present, well-negotiated).

| Cost Item | Traditional (Maquininha + PDV) | Dobar @ 8% Regular | Dobar @ 6% + R$199/mo Master |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly system / platform fee | R$300–450 (PDV + rental) | R$0 | R$199 |
| Payment processing fees | R$30k × 1.3% = ~R$390 | Included in commission | Included in commission |
| Marketing / customer acquisition | R$500–1,500/mo | Included (in-app discovery) | Included + ad credits |
| Hardware amortization | R$15–25/mo | R$0 | R$0 |
| **Total monthly cost** | **~R$1,205–2,365/mo** | **R$30k × 8% = R$2,400/mo** | **R$30k × 6% + R$199 = R$1,999/mo** |
| Revenue uplift from digital ordering (+30%) | None | R$9k extra GMV generated | R$9k extra GMV generated |
| **Effective cost on total GMV with uplift** | R$1,200–2,365 / R$30k = 4–8% | **R$2,400 / R$39k = 6.15%** | **R$1,999 / R$39k = 5.13%** |

> **The correct pitch to bar owners:** Dobar does not promise lower raw payment fees than a physical maquininha. It promises lower total cost of commerce: ordering system + customer acquisition + analytics + zero hardware — all included. At 8% on R$30k GMV, Dobar costs R$2,400 vs R$1,200–2,365 traditional — approximately breakeven on paper. But with a 30% volume uplift from frictionless digital ordering, the venue earns R$9,000 more GMV per month, making the effective rate 6.15% on total revenue — competitive with and often better than the traditional bundle.

## 4.3 Recommended Commission Structure

The original business plan (v1.0) proposed 18% Regular / 14% Master / 10% VIP. Based on Pagar.me gateway costs, competitive benchmarks against Goomer, and the iFood take rate positioning, the following revised structure is recommended:

| Tier | Commission Rate | Monthly Fee | Pagar.me Cost (blended ~3.6%) | Dobar Net Gross Margin | vs iFood Basic (15.2%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regular (free) | **8%** | R$0 | ~3.6% | ~4.4% gross margin per transaction | Dobar is **47% cheaper** than iFood Basic |
| Master | **6%** | R$199/mo | ~3.6% | ~2.4% + subscription revenue | Dobar is **61% cheaper** than iFood Basic |
| VIP | **4%** | R$599/mo | ~3.6% | ~0.4% + subscription revenue | Dobar is **74% cheaper** than iFood Basic |

### Why Lower Than the Original Business Plan?

**(a) Goomer charges zero commission.** At 18% Regular, a bar owner comparing Dobar to Goomer's R$699/month will not switch. At 8%, Dobar only becomes more expensive than Goomer at GMV above ~R$8,750/month — a realistic threshold for an active bar, making 8% a viable and sellable offer.

**(b) iFood's announced return at ~18% blurs the message.** If Dobar charges 18%, early adopters see no material difference from iFood Basic at 15.2%. The 8% rate creates a clear "half the cost of iFood" positioning that is easy to communicate in a single sentence.

**(c) VIP at 4% + R$599 works at scale.** A high-volume bar at R$80k/month: 4% = R$3,200 + R$599 = R$3,799 total. The old 10% VIP rate would cost R$8,599/month — pricing out exactly the flagship venues Dobar needs as social proof and anchor tenants.

## 4.4 Break-Even Analysis — Dobar vs Goomer (Closest In-Venue Competitor)

Goomer (merged with Abrahao) is the primary in-venue competitor: flat subscription only, zero commission, 400k restaurants on platform.

| Monthly GMV | Goomer Advanced (R$699/mo, 0% commission) | Dobar Regular (R$0/mo, 8%) | Dobar Master (R$199/mo, 6%) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R$5,000 | R$699 | R$400 | R$499 | **Dobar wins** |
| R$8,750 | R$699 | R$700 | R$724 | Breakeven Regular vs Goomer |
| R$10,000 | R$699 | R$800 | R$799 | Master ~= Goomer |
| R$15,000 | R$699 | R$1,200 | R$1,099 | Goomer cheaper on pure cost; Dobar value = consumer discovery |
| R$30,000 | R$699 | R$2,400 | R$1,999 | Goomer much cheaper on fee; Dobar value must be incremental revenue |
| R$80,000 | R$699 | R$6,400 | R$4,999 | Dobar VIP (R$3,799) wins vs Master/Regular — critical tier |

> **Strategic implication:** For venues doing R$15k–R$80k/month GMV, Goomer is cheaper on pure cost. Dobar must win on incremental revenue: the in-app consumer discovery brings NEW customers who find the bar through Dobar — something Goomer cannot do as it has no consumer-facing app. This must be Dobar's core sales story, backed by concrete beta venue data showing volume uplift.

## 4.5 Gateway Cost Floor — What Dobar Actually Pays (Pagar.me e-Commerce)

Understanding the cost floor is critical for margin management. All rates below are card-not-present (online API) — inherently higher than physical maquininha due to fraud risk.

| Payment Method | Mix (Typical Bar) | Pagar.me Rate | Fixed Fees | Total Cost on R$55 avg order | Effective % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PIX | 55% | 1.09% | R$0.55 | R$0.60 + R$0.55 = R$1.15 | 2.09% |
| Credit à vista | 35% | 3.19% | R$0.55 + R$0.44 (antifraude) = R$0.99 | R$1.75 + R$0.99 = R$2.74 | 4.98% |
| Debit (estimate) | 10% | ~1.5% | R$0.55 | R$0.83 + R$0.55 = R$1.38 | 2.50% |
| **Blended weighted average** | | | | | **~3.5–3.7%** |

Additional fixed fee: R$3.67 per bank transfer-out (settlement to venue), amortized weekly over batch — negligible per transaction at scale.

**At Dobar's 8% Regular commission:** net gross margin = 8% − 3.6% (blended gateway) = **~4.4%** before infrastructure, support, and sales costs. This is workable at scale and compresses toward breakeven only for very low-ticket venues (avg order < R$20).
