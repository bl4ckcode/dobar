## 6.1 Summary of Key Findings

Before prescribing actions, this section consolidates the critical findings from the full market analysis into a single reference table. Each finding has a direct implication for Dobar's immediate decisions.

| Item | Finding | Impact on Dobar |
|---|---|---|
| iFood payment model | 15.2–26.5% all-in take rate; own gateway iFood Pay; weekly settlement; Conta Digital for float revenue | Primary competitive benchmark — Dobar at 8% is dramatically cheaper and must own this narrative |
| Ze Delivery model | Zero commission to distribution partners; Ambev product margin model; no in-venue ordering platform | Not a competitor; evaluate as strategic Ambev/Ze Delivery partnership for B2B bar outreach |
| Top direct competitor | Goomer + Abrahao: flat fee R$40–699/mo, zero commission, 400k venues — but **no consumer discovery app whatsoever** | Dobar must win on customer acquisition value, not raw fee comparison; consumer app is the moat |
| Pagar.me e-commerce cost | ~3.5–3.7% blended (PIX 2.09% effective; credit 4.98% effective on R$55 avg order) | Commission must cover this floor; revenue model math must account for it from day 1 |
| Physical maquininha rates | Card-present 0.57–2.69% (lower than e-commerce) + R$300–500/mo PDV system cost | Do NOT claim lower transaction fees; claim lower total commerce cost + built-in growth |
| Advertising potential | 6 ad products viable; Brazilian local CPM R$8–25; CPH R$4–15; CPC R$1.20–2.50 | Advertising can reach 20–30% of total revenue at 1,000+ venues; bundle packages simplify selling |

## 6.2 Priority Action Items

Actions are organized into three priority tiers: **P0** (pre-launch blockers), **P1** (first 90 days), and **P2** (quarter 2–3 of operations).

---

### P0 — Pre-Launch Blockers (Must resolve before first venue onboarding)

**P0.1 — Revise Commission Rates**

Update all commission structures from the original business plan (18% / 14% / 10%) to the revised rates (8% / 6% / 4%) across all tiers.

- Update investor materials and pitch deck
- Update in-app pricing screens and onboarding flow
- Update all legal/contractual templates
- Update the business plan financial model with corrected unit economics

**Why it's P0:** If the first venue signs at 18%, it will be nearly impossible to renegotiate. Getting the pricing right from day one is non-negotiable. Early adopters will become references — they must experience the correct commercial terms.

---

**P0.2 — Reframe the Marketing Pitch**

The current pitch implies lower payment fees vs maquininha on a raw transaction basis — this claim is false for e-commerce modality and legally/commercially dangerous if a venue attempts to verify it.

The correct pitch is: **"Lower total cost of commerce, with built-in growth."**

- Remove any claim comparing Dobar's e-commerce rates to physical maquininha transaction rates
- Replace with: Total Cost of Ownership framing (Section 4.2 above)
- Key message: "Half the cost of iFood with zero delivery dependency and zero upfront hardware"
- Train any sales team or partner onboarding staff on this distinction before any B2B conversations

---

### P1 — First 90 Days

**P1.1 — Build 3 Beta Venue Case Studies**

Dobar's core claim — that digital ordering increases GMV by 25–40% — requires proof. Without it, the value proposition vs Goomer is theoretical. With it, it becomes a compelling sales tool.

What to measure:
- Average order value before and after Dobar adoption
- Order volume (daily/weekly) before and after
- Queue wait time reduction (qualitative, venue staff interview)
- Net Promoter Score of venue staff after 60 days

Format the output as a one-page PDF case study per venue, with specific numbers. Even a 20% GMV uplift from a single real venue creates a repeatable sales asset worth more than any marketing spend.

**Owner:** Product + Customer Success  
**Timeline:** Q3 2026 — concurrent with beta venue rollout

---

**P1.2 — Negotiate Volume Discount with Pagar.me**

The current blended gateway rate of ~3.5–3.7% is the primary margin pressure point. At VIP tier (4% commission), there is almost no gross margin left after gateway costs. Every 0.5% negotiated off the gateway rate translates directly to additional gross profit.

Negotiation target: **blended rate below 2.5%** (achievable at R$500k+ monthly GMV with Pagar.me). At R$500k GMV:
- Current rate (3.6%): R$18,000/month in gateway costs
- Target rate (2.5%): R$12,500/month in gateway costs
- Saving: R$5,500/month → R$66,000/year in recovered margin

Actions:
- Request a formal volume pricing schedule from Pagar.me at R$100k, R$250k, R$500k, R$1M GMV thresholds
- Document the target rate in the investor materials as a known value-creation lever
- In parallel, evaluate whether Adyen (at scale) or Efí Bank (lower-volume PIX) could serve as a secondary or primary processor at better blended rates (see Q1 payment processor analysis)

**Owner:** CEO / Finance  
**Timeline:** Begin negotiation at R$100k/month GMV

---

**P1.3 — Launch 3 Advertising Bundle Packages at Go-Live**

Do not launch advertising products in full catalog complexity. Bar owners are not digital marketers. Launch exactly three bundles at go-live:

1. Starter Boost (R$99/mo) — positioned as "try it for less than a birthday dinner"
2. Happy Hour Blaster (R$149/mo) — for venues with happy hour already running
3. Weekend Warrior (R$249/mo) — for venues with Friday/Saturday as peak nights

Do NOT lead with individual CPM/CPC/CPH products in the onboarding flow. Surface them only after a venue has purchased a bundle and wants to customize.

**Owner:** Product  
**Timeline:** Q3 2026 launch

---

### P2 — Quarters 2–3

**P2.1 — Evaluate Ambev / Ze Delivery Strategic Partnership**

Ambev has existing commercial relationships with virtually every bar and restaurant in Brazil. Ze Delivery has the distribution infrastructure and brand recognition in the beer category. A formal partnership — even a pilot — could unlock B2B sales reach that no sales team could replicate organically.

Potential partnership structures to evaluate:
- **Co-marketing:** Ze Delivery promotes Dobar to bar partners via their account manager network in exchange for cross-promotion of Ze Delivery in the Dobar consumer app
- **Exclusive promotional category:** Ambev brands (Brahma, Skol, Stella) get preferential placement in Dobar's "Trending Drinks" home section
- **Data partnership:** Dobar shares anonymized consumption data (drink category popularity by neighborhood) with Ambev in exchange for commercial introductions

**Important constraint:** Any Ambev partnership negotiation must be conducted through Dobar Tecnologia as an independent company, not through any employment relationship with CI&T or ABI US (see Bonus Q4 guidance).

**Owner:** CEO / Partnerships  
**Timeline:** Q4 2026

---

**P2.2 — Competitive Intelligence Monitoring on Goomer**

Goomer (merged with Abrahao) is the most dangerous competitor because it operates at zero commission and has 400k venues. Any meaningful pricing move from Goomer — raising flat fees above R$200/month, launching a consumer app, or raising external capital — should trigger a Dobar response.

Set up a monthly competitive intelligence review covering:
- Goomer pricing page changes
- App store review mining (what venues complain about → Dobar differentiation opportunities)
- LinkedIn job postings (signal of capability expansion)
- Press coverage and funding news

If Goomer raises its Advanced tier above R$699/month → Dobar's Regular tier (R$0 + 8%) becomes competitively superior for venues up to R$8,750 GMV, which includes a large segment of the market. This is a meaningful commercial trigger.

**Owner:** CEO  
**Timeline:** Monthly review, starting immediately

## 6.5 The Single Most Important Decision Before Launch

**Choose the right anchor claim and stick with it.**

Every sales conversation, pitch deck slide, and onboarding screen must answer the same question the bar owner is asking: *"Why Dobar instead of what I have today?"*

The answer that is both true and differentiated:

> *"Dobar replaces your PDV system, your marketing budget, and your customer acquisition with a single platform at 8% of what your customers spend — no upfront cost, no hardware, no contract. When iFood charges restaurants 26% to deliver food, we charge 8% to bring customers through your door."*

Everything else is supporting evidence for that claim.
